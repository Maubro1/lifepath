# Stage 7 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** No gaps on short waits, see-through and lag-free fog, lines that never change shape, line-following heat, a glass map screen, modes hidden from the UI, and transit trips snapped to their OSM routes.

**Architecture:** Logic in Core with Windows tests (motion engine, Visvalingam importances, heat ramp, mode pass, route matching). The trip-line cache becomes the one source the map draws at every zoom, holding street- and route-snapped geometry. Rendering stays three long-lived `MKOverlayRenderer` layers plus a heat layer; UI follows iOS 26 system glass.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6, SwiftUI, MapKit, GRDB, XCTest, XcodeGen, GitHub Actions (simulator screenshots via `[screens]`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-stage-7-design.md` (evidence: `reports/LifePath stage 7 techniques.md`)

## Global Constraints

- Power stop: 2 min inside a 20–100 m fence (6 min after a vehicle, as now); for the first 15 min of a stop away from a known place, spot checks every 15 s and the wake threshold stays 15 steps. Diary visits need 10 min (`Places.visits` minDuration 600, unchanged).
- Fog 82% opacity via `context.setAlpha`/colour alpha inside `draw`; never `renderer.alpha`.
- Lines: Visvalingam effective areas, monotone; keep vertices whose area ≥ (1 screen pt)² at the tile's zoom; trip ends always kept. Trip-line cache tolerance 3 m.
- Heat: additive strokes into a quarter-resolution buffer per tile, blur once, `log(1+n)/log(1+max)` with the diary's max per zoom, 256-entry ramp transparent → mustard → satsuma → cherry → light core.
- Modes never appear in UI; unclear motorised pieces are `.vehicle`.
- Rounded continuous corners: cards 12 pt, floating panels 22 pt; map controls in system glass.
- Overpass: index per tile, geometry per relation once; only rectangles and ids leave the phone.
- Every UI change: `[screens]` run, inspect, send the owner screenshots, wait for feedback before tagging.

## Review Focus

1. A 3-minute platform wait then walking on: the track restarts within ~15 s of moving, no gap; at home the old backoff still prevents potter wakes.
2. Zooming in and out over a trip: the line keeps its shape (vertex sets nest); a live trip still extends.
3. Light/Dark switch with fog and heat visible: both redraw in the new appearance.
4. A train ride where the route relation is missing or the stations are not in order: the ride keeps its rail matching; nothing is snapped to a wrong line.
5. Old diaries: trip lines rebuild once at 3 m with snapped geometry; nothing is lost if the rebuild is interrupted.
