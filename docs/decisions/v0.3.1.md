# v0.3.1 decisions

Decisions taken on the owner's behalf while building "tracking under pressure" (spec 2026-11-16-tracking-under-pressure-design), with what each costs if wrong.

- Ruling: fixes above the accuracy ceiling are stored as kind `rough` (raw, at most one per 10 s) and never enter the live filter; older app versions read them as moving — cost if wrong: a downgrade draws rough stretches as ordinary lines
- Ruling: rough points never split a trip by a jump, never count as heat, and trip distance runs only between precise points — cost if wrong: a trip with only rough points shows 0 km until stage 3 matching
- Ruling: the smoother weighs a rough stretch by its own consistency (second differences), clamped to 20 m … hAcc; spikes of ≤ 3 fixes within 30 s get negligible weight; walking pace from the pedometer (7-day history) acts as speed where Doppler is missing; smoothing version 2 re-smooths every trip once — cost if wrong: retune the thresholds from health reports
- Ruling: pottering vs leaving uses walked distance, drift of the median position and noise (potter: W ≥ 40 m and D ≤ N + 10; leave: D beyond the rest area + N + 15 m twice in a row and growing); the backoff resets after 30 min of rest — cost if wrong: retune
- Ruling: at a visible pin a steps wake waits for a keep-alive reading outside the shape, the geofence, or 3 min of walking pace; steps older than 5 min no longer count; the keep-alive wake is a wander — cost if wrong: the first tens of metres after leaving home can stay a gap
- Ruling: the departure geofence at a known place sits on the pin with the shape's extent + 55 m (at least 200 m) — cost if wrong: a slightly later geofence wake at small places
- Ruling: struggle mode (hundred-metre GPS, 50 m distance filter, 20 s probe every 5 min) after 3 min of only rough fixes in a vehicle; a rough fix farther than max(300 m, 2 × hAcc) from the last precise fix counts as travelling, because CoreMotion often reads trains as stationary — cost if wrong: a 2 km+ cell error at home could start a false ride
- Ruling: only automotive uses `.otherNavigation`; cycling keeps `.fitness` — cost if wrong: none measurable yet
- Ruling: the map reloads live at most every 15 s while visible, diffs overlays by key, snaps detail to powers of two from 3 m, and draws rough stretches dashed — cost if wrong: none
